Privacy Policy for Cube Run

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Cube Run app for Android, published on Google Play by the Cube Run developer.

Information the app itself stores

Cube Run does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your settings, high score and achievements are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

Cube Run shows advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from IP, and app interaction data. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before personalised advertising or other local storage is used, and you can change your choice at any time from the consent prompt.

For details, see Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and Google's advertising technologies page: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Google provides us only with confirmation of whether the purchase is active. Removing the app and reinstalling restores a purchase you still own.

Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app can read your Play Games player name, avatar and level, and can send achievement progress. This is optional; the game is fully playable without signing in. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to your Play Games account.

Analytics

The web version of the game loads Google Tag Manager, which may set cookies and record aggregate usage such as page views and basic device information. The Android app uses it for crash and traffic diagnostics. This data is not used to identify you personally.

Children

Cube Run is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • Withdraw or change advertising consent from the in-app consent prompt.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop advertising entirely.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data.

Data deletion requests

Because the app does not hold an account for you, there is no server-side profile to delete. If you want a data deletion request recorded anyway, contact the developer using the email address on the Google Play listing and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions or requests: use the developer contact address on the Cube Run Google Play listing.
